## List of Problem Statements

### 1. Linh – Independent Traveler

> **Problem:**
> Independent travelers spend 10–15 hours collecting, filtering, and organizing spots from multiple sources because no tool connects their favorite spots with an actual plan.
> **→ Need:** a solution that reduces planning time while maintaining flexibility and personalization.

---

### 2. Tuấn – Group Planner

> **Problem:**
> Group trip organizers struggle to compile, share, and align itineraries because current tools (Excel, My Maps, chat) are not integrated and lack clear task assignment.
> **→ Need:** an interactive trip map with timeline and to-do features for each member.

---

### 3. Vy – Collaborator

> **Problem:**
> Invited group members often feel passive and unaware of updates or changes due to fragmented communication across chat, sheets, and maps.
> **→ Need:** a lightweight, intuitive interface to view, respond, and give feedback easily without confusion.

---

### 4. Mai – Experienced Traveler

> **Problem:**
> Frequent travelers lack a tool to store, reuse, and share past trips; their data is scattered across multiple apps.
> **→ Need:** a system that allows managing and reusing old plans as detailed “trip templates.”

---

### 5. Huy – Guest Viewer

> **Problem:**
> People who only view shared trip links find them dry, confusing, and lacking context or emotion.
> **→ Need:** a contextual, visual, and emotional trip-viewing mode with timeline and photos — no account required.

---

### 6. Phương – Spot Curator

> **Problem:**
> People who love saving spots have no central place to organize, tag, or record reasons for saving; data is scattered (Maps, Notes, Instagram).
> **→ Need:** a place to store, categorize, and quickly reuse saved spots when creating new trips.

---

### 7. Khánh – Spot Evaluator

> **Problem:**
> People who rely on reviews to choose spots must switch between multiple apps to verify information, leading to fatigue and distrust.
> **→ Need:** a unified review and recommendation system to compare and verify spot reliability before saving or visiting.

---

### 8. Ngọc – Local Contributor

> **Problem:**
> Locals who want to share authentic places or travel tips lack a friendly, easy-to-use, and trustworthy platform where they are recognized.
> **→ Need:** a simple, reliable tool to post accurate spots and reviews with photos and “local verification.”

---

### 9. Hải – Route Explorer

> **Problem:**
> Road trippers or explorers have no way to record their real route (GPS, photos, stops) without drawing manually.
> **→ Need:** an automated route-recording solution that saves and shares routes as living maps.

---

### 10. Duy – System Curator

> **Problem:**
> As community-generated data grows, verification, merging, and quality maintenance become increasingly difficult and time-consuming.
> **→ Need:** an intelligent moderation tool to detect duplicates, merge, and label data efficiently.

---

### 11. Quân – Data Integrator

> **Problem:**
> Data from various sources (Tourism Departments, public APIs, crawlers) is inconsistent and slow to update, causing errors and inaccuracy.
> **→ Need:** an automated system for monitoring, standardizing, and syncing data to ensure freshness and consistency.

---

### 12. Thảo – Recommendation Designer

> **Problem:**
> Current recommendations often lack emotional depth and fail to reflect real behavior, leading users to ignore or distrust them.
> **→ Need:** a smart recommendation engine combining behavioral and emotional context data to deliver “right-time, right-mood” suggestions.

---

### 13. Trang – Community Builder

> **Problem:**
> Travel content creators lack a platform to visually share real journeys and track their impact (who views, saves, or follows their trips).
> **→ Need:** a storytelling tool with maps and emotions, plus recognition systems (badges, verified, top creator) to motivate contribution and community growth.

---

### 14. Long – Loyal Member

> **Problem:**
> Users only return to the app during trips and lack long-term motivation due to missing rewards, memories, or personal connection.
> **→ Need:** a system that preserves and recalls travel memories (recap, badges, points) to build habit and emotional attachment to the platform.

## 📊 **Quick Summary by Role Group**

| Group                    | Persona                  | Main Problem                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Consumers**            | Linh, Tuấn, Vy, Mai, Huy | Difficult, fragmented, and emotionless trip planning.                             |
| **Curators / Reviewers** | Phương, Khánh            | No reliable place to save, evaluate, and reuse trusted spots.                     |
| **Producers**            | Ngọc, Hải                | Lack of easy tools to share or capture real travel data.                          |
| **System Layer**         | Duy, Quân, Thảo          | Challenges in moderation, synchronization, and intelligent recommendation design. |

---

## 💡 **Overall Insight (Big Picture)**

> - All 12 personas form **3 tourism information loops**:
>   1️⃣ **Planning Loop** – Linh, Tuấn, Vy, Huy  
>   2️⃣ **Exploring Loop** – Hải, Ngọc, Khánh, Phương  
>   3️⃣ **Reflection Loop** – Mai, Duy, Quân, Thảo  
>   4️⃣ **Loyalty Loop** – Trang, Long  
>   Each loop generates data (spot, route, trip, story) that the next loop uses to learn, recommend, and retain users → forming the system’s **Knowledge & Loyalty Flywheel**.
