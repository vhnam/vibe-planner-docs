## **1. OVERVIEW**

Analyzing the “Future State” aims to:

## **3. FUTURE STATE ACROSS 6 BABOK PERSPECTIVES**

### **3.1. Business Process**

**From:**

- Manual, fragmented process; users must switch between multiple tools (Google Maps, Notes, Facebook Groups, Excel, etc.).

**To:**

- A **unified, automated workflow**:

  1. Suggest destinations based on preferences & schedule
  2. Plan by day, with group collaboration
  3. Automatically optimize routes & budget
  4. Export plans, share and receive feedback
  5. Post-trip review storage
  6. Verified reviews feed back into the system

🟢 **Outcome:** Current planning process of 1–2 days → reduced to 4–5 hours.

## **4. FUTURE VALUE MODEL**

```
Input:
  User intent + travel preferences + budget + time
       ↓
Processing:
  AI Recommendation Engine + Community Data + Verified Reviews
       ↓
Output:
  Personalized itinerary + real-time collaboration + trusted experience
       ↓
Feedback:
  Post-trip review → improves recommendation accuracy → strengthens community trust
```

✨ **Value Loop:**
