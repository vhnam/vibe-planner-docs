## **1. OVERVIEW OF THE CURRENT STATE**

**Development Status:**

- No product yet – **greenfield project** (starting from scratch)
- **Stakeholders**, **pain points**, and **value gap** clearly identified
- Currently in the **pre-MVP** stage, defining product strategy and development roadmap

**Market Context:**

- Vietnam’s domestic tourism is growing **15–20% per year**, strongly recovering post-COVID
- Market spending scale: **$10–15 billion/year**, mainly in the **18–45 age group (~40–45 million people)**
- **>70%** smartphone penetration → favorable for mobile app adoption
- The trend of **self-planned trips** is rapidly increasing, especially among young users
- The government is promoting **digital transformation in tourism** and **sustainable travel**

**Competitive Landscape:**

- No Vietnamese platform offering an **“all-in-one”** travel planning solution
- International players (TripAdvisor, Traveloka, Google Travel) focus mainly on booking, not on planning or collaboration
- Market opportunity expanding, but **“window of opportunity” is only 2–3 years**

---

## **2. KEY PAIN POINTS (CURRENT STATE)**

### **2.1. Travel Planning Process – Fragmented & Inefficient**

| Problem                                | Impact                            | Root Cause                              |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Using 5–10 different tools to plan     | Takes 10–15h per trip             | No all-in-one tool, lack of integration |
| Unstructured and scattered information | Stress, data loss, duplication    | No templates or organizational system   |
| Difficult group coordination           | 80% of groups face version issues | No real-time collaboration tools        |

---

### **2.2. Lack of Trustworthy Review Sources**

| Problem                                      | Impact                           | Root Cause                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Google/Facebook contain many fake reviews    | 55% of users don’t trust reviews | No verification or moderation system |
| TripAdvisor doesn’t reflect Vietnamese views | 30% of experiences mismatched    | Not localized for Vietnam market     |
| Sponsored blogs/content create noise         | 2–3h verifying 3–5 sources       | No true traveler community           |

---

### **2.3. Post-Trip – Knowledge Wasted**

| Problem                          | Impact                          | Root Cause                          |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| 85% of users don’t write reviews | Data and experience loss        | No incentive or sharing mechanism   |
| Reviews short, lack detail       | Low reference value             | No gamification or personal profile |
| Knowledge stays in small circles | Community data feedback missing | No central hub for sharing          |

## **9. CURRENT STATE CONCLUSION**

> **Vietnam’s travel market is at a “golden moment”** for the emergence of a smart travel planning and recommendation platform tailored for Vietnamese users.
>
> The current problems (fragmented tools, lack of trust, no collaboration, wasted post-trip knowledge) create a **large Value Gap**, translating into a **strong product opportunity**.
>
> However, **time is the decisive factor** — the startup must **launch an MVP within the first 5 months**, focusing on:
>
> 1. **Reducing travel planning time by 50%**
> 2. **Building a reliable and verified review system**
> 3. **Activating the first core user community**
