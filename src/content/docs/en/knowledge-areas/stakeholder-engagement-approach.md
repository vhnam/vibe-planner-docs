## **1. PURPOSE**

The purpose of the **Stakeholder Engagement Approach** is to:

- Identify **who the stakeholders are**, their **level of influence**, **interests**, and **how to engage effectively** with each group.
- Ensure **collaboration, alignment, and long-term commitment** of stakeholders throughout the entire project lifecycle.
- Maintain a **two-way communication flow** — ensuring that information, decisions, and feedback are continuously shared and responded to.

> 🎯 **Expected outcome:** Stakeholders clearly understand their roles, feel heard, and actively participate throughout the product's formation – testing – scaling phases.

---

## **2. PRINCIPLES**

| Principle                       | Description of Application                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Empathy & Transparency**      | All product decisions are clearly communicated to relevant stakeholders.                |
| **Mutual Value Creation**       | Every interaction brings value to both sides (user insight ↔ product improvement).      |
| **Continuous Engagement**       | Engagement is not “one and done”, but involves consistent feedback loops.               |
| **Right Level, Right Time**     | Avoid over-involving stakeholders when not necessary — optimize time & energy.          |
| **Data-Informed Communication** | All discussions and prioritizations are based on data (analytics, survey, market data). |

## **4. STAKEHOLDER ANALYSIS**

| Group                | Engagement Goal              | Expectations                       | Risks if Not Engaged         | Mitigation Strategy                     |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| **End Users**        | Good experience, useful app  | Easy-to-use, personalized features | Loss of adoption, high churn | Regular feedback loop, beta testing     |
| **Founders / PM**    | Product success              | ROI, growth metrics                | Priority conflict            | Clearly aligned KPIs & OKRs             |
| **Engineering Team** | Clear, feasible requirements | Well-defined, stable scope         | Delays, misunderstandings    | Agile syncs, backlog grooming           |
| **Local Businesses** | Profit, traffic              | User reach, analytics access       | Lack of participation        | Share data insights, marketing support  |
| **Tourism Boards**   | Promote tourism              | Accurate reports, data access      | No data support              | Provide dashboards + periodic reporting |
| **Investors**        | Financial performance        | Progress, growth plans             | Lack of transparency         | Monthly reports + KPI dashboard         |
| **Influencers**      | Community & reputation       | Visibility, rewards                | Lack of sharing motivation   | Partnership reward program              |

## **5. ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY**

### **5.1. By Product Lifecycle Stage**

| Stage                  | Engagement Objective                 | Specific Actions                                |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Discovery**          | Understand user needs and behaviors  | User interviews, surveys, community workshops   |
| **Design**             | Validate ideas & prototypes          | Co-creation sessions, prototype testing         |

| **Growth & Operation** | Improve experience, retain users     | Feedback loop, NPS tracking, community building |
| **Evaluation**         | Measure created value                | KPI reporting, retrospective reviews            |

## **8. ENGAGEMENT MONITORING & ADJUSTMENT**

| Metric                     | Purpose                       | Tracking Tool            | Alert Threshold                       |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| **NPS Score**              | Measure user loyalty          | Typeform / In-app survey | < 30 → needs action                   |
| **Partner Satisfaction**   | Measure collaboration quality | Quarterly survey         | < 3.5/5 → review partnership policies |
| **Meeting Attendance**     | Track internal engagement     | Slack / Notion log       | < 70% attendance                      |
| **Feedback Response Rate** | Measure user interaction      | GA4 + Hotjar             | < 25% → improve survey UI             |
| **Decision Latency**       | Decision-making efficiency    | Jira / Timeline          | > 5 days delay → process improvement  |

## **9. ESCALATION & CONFLICT MANAGEMENT**

| Scenario                                            | Handling Method                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Priority conflict between Product & Engineering** | Use Impact/Effort matrix to decide                           |
| **Conflicting feature requests from stakeholders**  | Compare with Business Objectives, prioritize higher ROI path |
| **Negative public feedback from users**             | PM responds publicly, acknowledges & addresses transparently |
| **Partners unhappy with data/reports**              | Involve them in dashboard co-design & data validation        |

## **11. ENGAGEMENT RISKS & MITIGATION**

| Risk                    | Impact                            | Mitigation Strategy                             |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Stakeholders overlooked | Lack of buy-in, future opposition | Keep Stakeholder Register regularly updated     |
| Communication overload  | Loss of focus, burnout            | Prioritize info, use asynchronous tools         |
| Lack of user feedback   | Assumptions unvalidated           | Incentivize feedback via rewards or mini-games  |
| Lack of transparency    | Loss of trust                     | Public OKRs + progress dashboard                |
| Stakeholder turnover    | Loss of continuity                | Document all decisions & maintain contact chain |

---

## **12. SUMMARY**

> This project's **Stakeholder Engagement Approach** is designed to be **co-creative, data-driven, and relationship-based**.
> Rather than simply “managing stakeholders”, the team actively builds a **co-creation community** — where each party contributes to value creation.

**Philosophy Summary:**

- 👂 _Listen actively_ — Hear before explaining
- 🤝 _Engage continuously_ — Maintain relationships throughout the product lifecycle
- 📊 _Measure engagement_ — Go beyond communication to assess real impact
- 🧭 _Align to value_ — Every interaction should answer: “Which business goal does this support?”
