## **1. STRATEGIC OVERVIEW**

### **1.1. Current Position**

- **Market:** Vietnam’s domestic tourism is growing at 15–20%/year, with ~40 million people aged 18–45 — a segment with strong self-planning demand.
- **Gap:** No Vietnamese _all-in-one_ platform yet exists for planning, sharing, and collaboration.
- **Product stage:** **Pre-MVP** phase, with a strategy of “launch fast – learn fast – scale early.”
- **Core value:** Reduce planning time by 50%, increase information trust, and activate authentic sharing community.

### **1.2. Strategic Intent**

> **To become the first Vietnamese travel platform that helps people easily plan, discover, and share trips — powered by data, community, and trust.**

**Vision:**

> “Every Vietnamese person will think of our platform first when planning a trip.”

**Mission:**

> “To help Vietnamese people plan trips more easily, faster, and with greater confidence — through an all-in-one platform that combines technology and community.”

| --------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| **Adoption**    | Users slow to change habits          | Simple UX, onboarding & referral   |
| **Trust**       | Lack of initial content → cold start | Gamification + seed content        |
| **Competition** | Big tech expanding into planning     | Launch fast, focus on localization |
| **Resources**   | Small team, limited budget           | Prioritize MVP, lean operations    |
| **Timing**      | 2–3 year opportunity window          | MVP ≤ 5 months, fast scaling       |
