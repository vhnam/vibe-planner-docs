## 1. Stakeholder Identification

### 1.1. Identification Method

Using **3 main criteria** to identify stakeholders:

1. **Interest:** Level of concern about the project/solution
2. **Impact:** How they are affected by the change
3. **Influence:** Ability to affect project outcomes

**Metrics:**

| Metric                       | Target    | Measurement                           | Frequency       | Red Flag |
